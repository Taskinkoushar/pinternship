This is my first internship, and I had not done any internship before. Before joining this internship, I always thought that for doing an internship, complete knowledge of coding is needed, because after joining the internship, no basic knowledge will be provided and only a short overview will be given and then directly have to work on big projects. And because of this thinking I was afraid. I also thought that there will be people who will have knowledge about everything and how I will compete with them. And I expected the same thing with this internship as well.

when I joined this internship, I had many questions in my mind like how will I learn things from the beginning, will I be able to handle the projects or not and many more. Initially I thought that the internship will quickly move to projects, but after knowing that there was a proper learning phase challenged my earlier thinking at that time.

I did not have any prior knowledge of MERN Stack, which made the learning process difficult for me in the beginning. Another challenge for me is the proctoring process of the Vibe platform that was very difficult. Even small noise and movement caused the videos to restart again which frustrated me many times. However this experience forced me to concentrate properly on the lessons because without proper concentration, I can not pass the quiz and have to repeat the same process again. As I was learning everything from the beginning. I had to keep the concept of all the videos in mind and it was a bit difficult.

This internship changed my way of thinking on learning, I felt that if I don't know about something properly, I should not start it at all. At the beginning, I was afraid because I was thinking that whether i will be able to do it or not. Through this experience, I realised one thing that when you actually begin something, there the learning starts only, even if you are totally new to it or confused.

I also understood another important thing that there is no sense in comparing myself with others before starting. I was comparing myself with those people who had already started before me. Once I started learning consistently, I began to understand my potential that what I can do. This internship helped me to trust the learning process, even when it feels frustrating and to stay concentrated.
